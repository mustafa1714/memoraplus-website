About the project

187 commercial units designed for business, storage and light production, with advanced operating specifications and a strategic location near major companies, from $208,250. Flexible spaces combine storage, showrooms, offices and light production to give your business room to grow.

The project stands on 30,000 m² of land with up to 90,000 m² of built area and is developed by Hasanoğlu Şirketler Grubu and Çiftçi Modern Yapı, with infrastructure that helps companies organise operations and use space efficiently.

Specifications for commercial and industrial use

  • Ceiling height of up to 6 m, allowing vertical storage or a mezzanine subject to technical approvals.
  • Designed for truck movement between floors and access to each unit's loading doors.
  • Units are delivered Shell & Core, so buyers can plan and fit out the space for their activity within technical and regulatory requirements.

How can you use your unit?

  • Run your business: a head office, showroom, warehouse or light-production workshop, depending on each unit's licence.
  • Grow your business: storage, distribution and operations in one place.
  • Invest in commercial property: a unit may be let to a suitable company, depending on market demand and the conditions of use and letting.
  • Viability depends on the purchase price, fit-out and running costs, actual rental demand and ownership costs, so each unit should be assessed on its own.
