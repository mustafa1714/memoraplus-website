# Footer
The dark closing band of every page: logo, approved tagline, quick links, contact and social accounts.

**Consumer provides:** `memora-plus-logo-02-trimmed.webp` (approved for the dark ground as-is), the tagline, the five site links, contact rows (WhatsApp and phone now; email, address and map link when the client provides them), the three social links (Instagram, Facebook, YouTube) and the copyright line. Wrap in `.mp-dark` so focus rings and outline buttons switch.

**Rules:** ground `surface-dark`; column titles `accent-on-dark`; text `ink-on-dark`; secondary `ink-on-dark-muted`; dividers `border-on-dark`. Three columns from 760px of footer width, stacked below. The phone number is always LTR. Social links are text labels (no brand logos drawn).
